\(\alpha\) & Under-relaxation coefficient in the longitudinal fixed-point iteration.\\
\(\beta_{x,y}\) & Horizontal or vertical Twiss beta function.\\
\(\mathbf c\) & Conditioning vector supplied to a conditional model.\\
\(D_{x,y}\) & Horizontal or vertical dispersion function.\\
\(\delta\) & Relative momentum or energy deviation.\\
\(\varepsilon_{x,y}\) & Horizontal or vertical geometric emittance.\\
\(\eta\) & Phase-slip factor in longitudinal dynamics.\\
\(\mathcal G\) & Target evolution or solution operator acting on a function or beam distribution.\\
\(H_{ij}\) & Two-dimensional histogram or probability-mass representation of coordinates \(( \xi_{i} , \xi_{j} ) .\)\\
\(\mathcal L\) & Generic training objective or loss function.\\
\(\lambda\) & Normalized longitudinal line density.\\
\(m_i\) & Centroid of coordinate \(\xi_{i} .\)\\
\(\mu\) & Vector of machine, lattice, material, or collective-effect conditioning parameters.\\
\(N_b\) & Number of histogram bins per coordinate.\\
\(N_p\) & Number of macroparticles per beam cloud.\\
\(N_s\) & Number of simulated input–output samples or trajectories.\\
\(N_\zeta\) & Number of points in the longitudinal discretization grid.\\
\(\Phi_\mu\) & Single-particle or macroparticle-cloud transport map for parameters \(\mu .\)\\
\(p_\psi(X\mid z,c)\) & Conditional decoder distribution parameterized by \(\psi .\)\\
\(p_x\) & Horizontal normalized canonical momentum.\\
\(p_y\) & Vertical normalized canonical momentum.\\
\(Q\) & Quality factor of a resonator model.\\
\(q_\phi(z\mid X,c)\) & Approximate conditional latent posterior parameterized by encoder parameters \(\phi .\)\\
\(R\) & Number of stochastic posterior samples used in a Monte Carlo ensemble.\\
\(\rho\) & Beam probability density in six-dimensional phase space.\\
\(\rho^N\) & Empirical beam measure formed from a finite macroparticle cloud.\\
\(\sigma_i\) & Coordinate-wise RMS spread of coordinate \(\xi_{i} .\)\\
\(W_1\) & Wasserstein-1 distance.\\
\(W_\parallel\) & Longitudinal wake function.\\
\(W_\perp\) & Transverse wake function.\\
\(x\) & Horizontal transverse position.\\
\(\xi\) & Six-dimensional phase-space coordinate, typically \(\xi = ( x , p_{x} , y , p_{y} , \zeta , \delta ) .\)\\
\(y\) & Vertical transverse position.\\
\(\zeta\) & Longitudinal position relative to the synchronous particle.\\
\(\mathbf z\) & Latent random variable or encoded latent state.\\
\(Z_\parallel\) & Longitudinal beam coupling impedance.\\
\(Z_\perp\) & Transverse beam coupling impedance.\\
\end{longtable}
